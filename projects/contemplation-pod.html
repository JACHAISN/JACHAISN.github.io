Project 01

Contemplation Pod

Medium
Concept Design, Physical Model
Tools
Rhino 8, Hand-Sketching, Laser Cutting
Reference
Einstein's Dreams

Contemplation Pod is based on Einstein's Dreams, exploring a dream logic in which time slows the farther one moves from the earth. The governing concept is “ants to elephants”: near the ground, forms are small, fast, and dense — chaotic and crowded, like ants. Moving upward through the pod, forms thicken, enlarge, and spread apart, becoming slower and calmer, like elephants.

The physical progression through the structure represents this shift — from rapid, crowded movement at the base to stillness and contemplation at its upper reaches. Scale, density, and pace are used as spatial tools, so that ascending the pod is itself an experience of time dilating.
